Production workflow and glossary

From script to final cut, shot design, checks and common terms.

23. A practical production workflow#

For a one-minute explainer, start with a short script. Split it into sections, and give each section one core image: an icon, a simple diagram, a short line of text or a chart.

In NewScribe, place the elements in section order first. Do not spend time perfecting positions before the order is stable. Then set appearance modes, hold times and camera positions.

Preview the whole project and check:

  • whether elements appear too quickly
  • whether camera moves are too frequent
  • whether text is readable on a phone
  • whether masks cover content they should not
  • whether audio and picture line up

Export a 720p or 1080p test first. Once it looks right, export the final resolution.

Splitting large projects#

If a project becomes very large, split it into several projects. Keep the background and visual style consistent, then combine the exported videos in a video editor. This is often more reliable than maintaining a single enormous timeline.

Shot design#

One shot should carry one information task. If a sentence contains two parallel ideas, use two elements or two shots. If an image is too detailed, zoom into a part or reveal it with a mask instead of showing everything at once.

Avoid changing the camera too often. The viewer needs time to read and understand the frame.

Asset style#

Use SVG when the content is a clean icon, outline or diagram. Use a bitmap for a photograph or texture. Keep line weights, colours and illustration styles consistent across the project.

Final check#

Check spelling, missing assets, masks, font fallback, audio length, video duration and output size. Play the exported file once from beginning to end before publishing.

The first video made with NewScribe#

“佛山行（一）【临高启明第三卷12】” (Foshan Journey, Part 1) was the first video made with NewScribe. Watch it here:

佛山行（一）【临高启明第三卷12】 · Bilibili

The video was edited in post-production. Transitions, subtitles and some other effects were not made in NewScribe; NewScribe provided the whiteboard animation, vector drawing, hands, camera work and base picture. Treat it as a reference for the overall style, not as proof that every effect in the video is a NewScribe feature.

24. Glossary#

SVG
Scalable Vector Graphics. A path-based image format that stays sharp when scaled.

Bitmap
An image made of pixels, such as PNG or JPG.

Vector draw
Drawing along the paths and strokes of an SVG.

Bitmap draw
Creating a scribble or paint path from image pixels.

Camera / camera pose
The position and zoom used to view a scene when an element appears.

Mask
An element that covers or reveals a region. Masks can affect all elements, previous elements only, or one specific element.

Composite fade
A fade combined with movement, zoom, spin or unfold.

Custom hand
A hand image imported by the user, with a configurable pen tip and height.

Share project
A .nspkg single-file project containing its assets and custom hands.
