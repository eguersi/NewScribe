Audio and video information

Two tracks, volume and fades, plus the video information written at export.

17. Audio and video information#

Background music#

You can import background music and adjust volume, fade in, fade out, loop and play once.

A second audio track is available for narration or an extra sound layer. NewScribe supports at most two tracks and does not provide audio trimming or detailed audio editing.

Mixing#

The two tracks are mixed together. They are not a master/subordinate pair, so control each volume separately to avoid one covering the other.

If both tracks are loud, the mix can clip or distort. Lower one track or reduce the overall level.

Audio length and video length#

If the audio is longer than the picture, the extra part is cut off. The export dialog warns about this.

If the audio is shorter, the video may end in silence. You can extend the final element’s hold or loop the music when appropriate. Loop is suitable for background music, not for narration with a definite ending.

Video information#

Before export you can fill in:

  • Title
  • Artist / author
  • Comment

The values are written into the MP4 metadata after export. If you do not change the information, NewScribe skips the extra metadata write.

Metadata appears in some players and media libraries. It does not appear on the video image.

Sync check#

Before export, check:

  • whether music enters at the right moment
  • whether narration matches the text
  • whether important cuts land on the intended beat
  • whether the ending feels natural
  • whether volume jumps unexpectedly
